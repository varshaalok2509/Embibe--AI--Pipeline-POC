# Embibe Content Gap — Leadership Decision Brief
**Prepared for:** JIO Leadership / CEO Review

## The One-Line Summary

> **We have mapped 12,885 content combinations across 35 boards, 25 grade levels, 224 subjects and 16 languages — but only 4.4% have full content. 90% of what we promised the market is still empty.**

---

## 1. The Market We Are Playing In (TAM)

India's K-12 education system is the largest in the world by student volume.

| Segment | Estimated Students | Strategic Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **CBSE** | ~20 million | National board; urban + aspirational India |
| **State Boards (combined)** | ~220 million | Bharat; vernacular India; JIO's core user base |
| **ICSE / Cambridge / IB** | ~5 million | Premium segment; high ARPU |
| **NIOS** | ~3 million | Open schooling; underserved |
| **Total K-12 India** | **~260 million** | Largest addressable base in the world |

Embibe has indexed **35 boards** and structured **12,885 unique content slots** — Board × Grade × Subject × Language. This is the content inventory we need to fill to serve this market.

---

## 2. Where We Stand Today — The Brutal Reality

| Status | Count | % of Total |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Full Coverage | 566 | **4.4%** |
| 🟡 Partial Coverage | 745 | **5.8%** |
| ❌ No Coverage | 11,574 | **89.8%** |

**9 out of every 10 content slots are empty.**

The platform has been structured but not filled. We have the taxonomy — we do not yet have the content.

### What "Partial" Actually Means
Of the 745 partial slots, the dominant gap type is:

- **81%** → Missing videos, lessons, and publisher books (603 slots)
- **4%** → Missing questions, videos, and lessons together (32 slots)
- **2%** → Only grammar content available (12 slots — English subject)
- **2%** → Limited to textbook questions + Gen-AI lessons only (14 slots)

This tells us the content that exists is shallow — questions may exist, but learning journeys (videos + lessons + practice) are not complete.
