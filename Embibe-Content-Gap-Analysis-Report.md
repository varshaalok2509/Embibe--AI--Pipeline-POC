# Embibe Content Coverage & Gap Analysis

**Prepared for:** Leadership Team  
**Date:** April 10, 2026  
**Data Source:** Board-Grade-Subject-Locale Coverage Matrix (12,885 combinations across 35 Boards, 25 Grades, 153 Subjects, 16 Locales)

---

## Executive Summary

Embibe's content library has **severe coverage gaps**. Out of 12,885 Board-Grade-Subject-Locale combinations:

| Status | Count | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **Full Coverage (Yes)** | 566 | **4.4%** |
| **Partial Coverage** | 745 | **5.8%** |
| **No Coverage** | 11,574 | **89.8%** |

The platform's content is heavily concentrated in **English-language, STEM subjects for Grades 6-12**. Nearly all non-English locales, non-STEM subjects, primary grades (1-5), and the entire NIOS catalog have zero content. This report identifies where to prioritize to maximize coverage impact.


---

## Key Insight #1: CBSE — Strong STEM Core, But Hindi and Non-STEM Are Empty

CBSE is Embibe's most critical board (largest student base nationally). Current state:

**What's covered (Full — English only):**
- Grades 1-2: Mathematics, Environmental Studies
- Grades 6-10: Mathematics, Science, Social Science
- Grades 11-12: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology

**What's partially covered:**
- English (language subject) across all grades — only in English locale
- Grades 11-12 Commerce/Humanities: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Geography, History, Political Science, Psychology — English locale only
- All core subjects in Hindi locale are Partial (videos, lessons, private publisher content missing)

**What's completely missing:**
- **Hindi-medium CBSE**: Zero full coverage. All 381 Hindi-locale entries are either Partial or No.
- **CBSE Grades 3-5**: No full coverage for any subject (only Partial for Math, EVS, English)
- **All non-STEM subjects** for Grades 1-10: Hindi (language), Sanskrit, Computer Education, Physical Education, Art, etc. — zero coverage
- Commerce/Humanities stream: Only partial, not full

### Decision Point for Leadership
> **Recommendation:** Completing CBSE Hindi-medium content for Math, Science, and Social Science (Grades 6-12) should be the single highest priority. This is the largest addressable audience with the least incremental effort — the English content already exists and can be localized. Estimated gap: ~38 subject-grade combos to move from Partial → Yes.

---

## Key Insight #2: NIOS Is the Largest Blind Spot (2,400 Entries, 0% Coverage)

NIOS (National Institute of Open Schooling) accounts for **18.6% of the entire dataset** (2,400 entries) and has **zero content** — not even partial.

- Covers 5 grade levels: Secondary Course, Senior Secondary Course, Level A/B/C
- Spans all 16 locales and 53 subjects
- NIOS serves ~3.5 million students annually, many from underserved backgrounds

### Decision Point for Leadership
> **Recommendation:** NIOS requires a dedicated strategy call. If the audience is strategically important (government partnerships, social impact mandates), prioritize Secondary & Senior Secondary in English and Hindi for core STEM subjects. If not a strategic priority, deprioritize entirely — the effort to cover 2,400 combinations is massive.

## Key Insight #4: Primary Grades (1-5) Are Almost Entirely Uncovered

| Grade Band | Total Entries | Full Coverage | Coverage % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grades 1-5 | 3,426 | 4 | **0.1%** |
| Grades 6-8 | 2,370 | 176 | **7.4%** |
| Grades 9-10 | 1,594 | 135 | **8.5%** |
| Grades 11-12 | 2,846 | 251 | **8.8%** |
| NIOS Levels (A/B/C/Sec/Sr Sec) | 2,400 | 0 | **0.0%** |
| International (IB/Cambridge) | 249 | 0 | **0.0%** |

Grades 1-5 have only **4 entries with full coverage** (CBSE Grades 1-2, Math & EVS, English only). Grades 3-5 have zero full coverage across all boards.

### Decision Point for Leadership
> **Recommendation:** If the product roadmap includes K-5 expansion, this is a greenfield build — not a gap-fill. Requires curriculum mapping from scratch for foundational literacy and numeracy. If Grades 6-12 are the strategic focus, deprioritize Grades 1-5 and redirect resources to deepening 6-12 coverage.

> - **Tier 3 (Later):** Smaller states and NE boards.

## Key Insight #6: Northeast Boards Have Surprisingly Higher Coverage Rates

Northeast state boards (Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim) collectively show:
- 9.0% full coverage (vs. 4.8% for major state boards)
- 11.9% partial coverage

This is because NE boards have fewer total subject-grade combinations, and the English-medium core subjects are covered. **Assam Board is the standout**: 47% partial coverage, driven by 191 Assamese-locale entries that are partially complete.

### Decision Point for Leadership
> **Recommendation:** Assam Board is the easiest "quick win" for regional language coverage. Converting 191 Assamese-partial entries to full coverage would make Assam the first state board with meaningful vernacular content — a strong case study for state government partnerships.

## Key Insight #7: What "Partial" Actually Means — Content Type Gaps

The Remarks column reveals what's missing in partially covered entries:

| Gap Type | Count | Share of Partial |
|---|---|---|
| Missing videos, lessons & private publisher books | 603 | **80.9%** |
| Missing questions, videos & lessons | 32 | 4.3% |
| Missing lessons & videos | 48 | 6.4% |
| Deficiency of videos, lessons & private publications | 21 | 2.8% |
| Limited to textbook questions + Gen-AI content only | 14 | 1.9% |
| Only grammar (language subjects) | 12 | 1.6% |
| Missing videos only | 5 | 0.7% |

**81% of partial coverage gaps are the same problem**: missing videos, lessons, and private publisher content. The platform has textbook-aligned questions but lacks the rich media and supplementary content.

### Decision Point for Leadership
> **Recommendation:** The dominant gap is not curriculum mapping — it's media production and publisher partnerships. Prioritize:
> 1. **Video lesson production** for the top 50 subject-grade-locale combos by student volume
> 2. **Private publisher licensing deals** to fill textbook content gaps at scale
> 3. **Gen-AI lesson generation** as a stopgap for lower-priority combos (already in use for 14 entries)

## Key Insight #8: International Boards (IB & Cambridge) Have Zero Full Coverage

| Board | Total | Yes | Partial | No |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IB | 123 | 0 | 18 (14.6%) | 105 |
| Cambridge Board | 126 | 0 | 14 (11.1%) | 112 |

Both boards are English-only and have partial coverage for some subjects, but no full coverage anywhere.

### Decision Point for Leadership
> **Recommendation:** International board students are a premium, high-ARPU segment. If the monetization strategy targets this segment, invest in IB Diploma and Cambridge IGCSE/AS-A Level content for core subjects (Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English). If not a revenue priority, deprioritize — the curriculum is specialized and expensive to produce.
