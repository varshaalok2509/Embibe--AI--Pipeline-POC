# Jio Education K-12 — vision and project map

## Vision

Jio Education is the school's stack. It runs school operations, delivers academic content, powers classroom delivery, and extends learning to the home — from one set of products, on one identity, with one billing relationship.

## The market

### Size and segments

India has 265 M K-12 students across 1.48 M schools (UDISE+ 2023-24). Of these, ~440 K are private schools serving ~110 M students — 42% of enrolment and the vast majority of school-tech spend. Total addressable school-tech wallet ~₹35K crore (the ₹8.6 lakh crore headline figure includes tuition, which is school income, not school spend).

Private K-12 splits into four fee tiers. The top tiers carry the bulk of school-tech spend; the bottom tiers carry most of the school count.

| Segment | Annual fees | Private schools | Students | Tech wallet / school / yr | Tech spend % of fees |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Affordable | < ₹1 L | ~310 K (70%) | ~55 M | ₹3–8 L | 2–5% |
| Mid-Market | ₹1–2 L | ~85 K (20%) | ~25 M | ₹10–25 L | 5–8% |
| Premium | ₹2–5 L | ~33 K (7–8%) | ~20 M | ₹30–60 L | 8–12% |
| Elite | ₹5 L+ | ~11 K (2–3%) | ~10 M | ₹80 L – 2 Cr | 10–15% |

Source: UDISE+ 2023-24, Ministry of Education; Central Square Foundation × Omidyar Network State of the Sector (2020) for fee-tier distribution.

### Why to move now

Four structural conditions — not created by Jio, but exploitable if we move now.

**School OS race is live.** LEAD, Teachmint and Tata ClassEdge are each racing to own the multi-product school contract. Nobody covers all four pillars across all four segments. That gap is the window.

**Free floor is real but beatable.** NCERT's DIKSHA and NISHTHA have reached 5 M+ teachers at no cost — NISHTHA alone has trained over 50 lakh teachers. Any paid play has to win on content depth, vernacular reach, or AI experience — not on price.

**AI has compressed content timelines.** Multi-year content programmes are now 12-month pipelines for teams that get generation and curation right. First-mover advantage is real.

**Policy compliance is unsolved.** NEP and PARAKH require outcome reporting that no legacy ERP handles natively. Schools without a solution pay in manual effort every cycle — the platform that closes this first earns a sticky wedge.

## Design principles

Five principles govern every product and project decision. A project that fails one needs a good reason.

1. **One school, one stack, one bill** — Single-vendor relationship preserved across all products. Any fragmentation — separate logins, separate invoices, separate contracts — needs an explicit justification.
2. **Sequence by impact, not by tier** — Affordable + Mid-Market hold ~73% of private students (80 M). Build and ship there first; Premium and Elite enhancements layer on top of the same core.
3. **Software decoupled from hardware** — Board software runs on BenQ, Samsung, ViewSonic, Promethean with full feature parity. Hardware ownership doesn't gate software adoption.
4. **AI native, not bolted on** — Content pipeline, AI tutor, and grading are generative at the architecture level. No after-the-fact AI layers.
5. **Win on depth and distribution, not on price** — Content depth, AI quality, and the Jio SIM distribution rail are the moats. Don't try to undercut DIKSHA's free floor.

## Priority projects — scope and outcomes

Eight projects in active build, derived from the matrices above. Numbering matches the project table.

---

**A1 — AI content pipeline**

Unify Embibe's existing pipeline, the Board curation capability, and external/NCERT sources into one production pipeline. CBSE 6/7 NCERT is the flagship proof point. Structured content and Q-bank only — no full AI video generation.
**Segments:** All · **Pillar:** Content & LMS

- Schools get board × grade × subject × topic gaps filled quickly.
- Done when: time-to-fill a new board × grade × subject drops below 8 weeks; all boards and grades live end-to-end, with CBSE 6/7 as the first proof point.

---

**B1 — TeachLite 2.0**

TeachLite 2.0 = Embibe + Superr software running on classroom hardware. Three SKUs:
- **Lite** — Smart TV + teacher phone (Affordable)
- **Standard** — IFP per wing, any OEM (Mid-Market)
- **Full** — IFP per classroom + Embibe content + Google Classroom integration (Premium / Elite)

Bundles Board classroom features (circle-to-search, whiteboard sharing, live assignments, OCR grading, AI lesson plans), Embibe content (Q-bank, video, AI tutor), and DigiCampus hooks (admissions, attendance, grading) where applicable.
**Segments:** All · **Pillar:** Teaching & Learning

- School signs one contract, gets one invoice; all four personas work inside Jio Education from day one.
- Teacher prep and grading time drops by at least half.
- Done when: ≥ 1 non-RFS school chain on the full bundle; teacher weekly active rate above 70%; bundle NPS above standalone Embibe Pack.

---

**H1 — Telco SIM-bundle distribution**

Every Jio SIM in a school-age household gets the Embibe student app and parent app preloaded at SIM activation. GPS targeting pushes appropriate language and board content by region.
**Segments:** All · **Pillar:** Telco SIM (cross-cutting)

- Consumer rail open; D2C user acquisition cost drops to near-zero.
- Done when: ≥ 1 lakh students acquired via the SIM-activation flow; per-acquisition cost validated against the app-store baseline.

---

**C1 — Embibe apps + 360, decluttered**

Full audit of every active Embibe app and 360 component → keep / merge / retire decision per surface. The homework loop (teacher assigns on Board → student submits on phone → parent sees progress) is built into the consolidated student app, not treated as a separate product. At least one user-visible consolidation ships, leaving two clean product lines: a B2B school pack (B1) and a direct-to-consumer surface.
**Segments:** All, including consumer/D2C · **Pillar:** Learn at Home + Teaching & Learning

- Students and parents get one app each; the homework cycle is visible in the same surface they already use.
- Jio gets two clean product lines (B2B school pack, D2C consumer) instead of four to five overlapping apps.
- Done when: every app has a keep/merge/retire decision; homework loop live in the consolidated student app; support tickets per active user down.

---

**A3 — LearnLite**

Offline, vernacular-first content for the Affordable segment. Maharashtra + Gujarat state boards as the first two launches. Content sized for low-end Android devices and intermittent connectivity.
**Segments:** Affordable · **Pillar:** Content & LMS

- Affordable schools get curriculum content without depending on broadband.
- Done when: ≥ 1 lakh Affordable students using LearnLite; Maharashtra + Gujarat state board coverage > 80%; daily active rate above 30%.

---

**A2 — Content team productivity tools**

Baseline the current process (items per content creator per month, QA cycle time, tools in use), identify bottlenecks, then ship ≥ 2 tools that remove repetitive steps — scripting aids, asset prep, review/QA automation. Output feeds the AI content pipeline (A1). Owner: Varsha Gajjar.
**Segments:** All · **Pillar:** Content & LMS

- Content team doubles items shipped per creator per month.
- QA cycle halves; reviewers stop being the bottleneck.
- Done when: items per content creator per month doubles against the measured baseline.

---

**B2 — AI Tutor reconciliation**

Superr's AI Companion runs on Embibe's backend. This project repurposes Superr's AI Companion as the Jio AI Tutor — one experience across the student app, TeachLite, and the Board. The Embibe AI Tutor track retires into it.
**Segments:** All · **Pillar:** Teaching & Learning + Learn at Home

- Students get one consistent AI Tutor regardless of which surface they open.
- Jio stops running two tutor roadmaps.
- Done when: Superr AI Companion live as the unified Jio AI Tutor across all surfaces; Embibe tutor deprecated; tutor engagement at or above pre-migration levels.

---

**G1 — Sales enablement toolkit**

Everything the sales team needs to close a school deal: segment-specific pricing sheets, demo kits, contract templates, billing/invoicing flows, partner-channel onboarding. Today this is a known sales blocker — TeachLite 2.0 GA depends on this shipping.
**Segments:** All · **Pillar:** Business Enablement

- Sales cycle time per school deal halves.
- Done when: full sales kit live across the four segment SKUs; ≥ 1 deal closed end-to-end through the new toolkit.

**A4 — Google Classroom integration**

**Segments:** Premium, Elite (where Google Workspace is entrenched) · **Pillar:** Content & LMS

- Schools running Google don't have to choose — they get TeachLite content and tools without leaving Classroom.
- Done when: bi-directional sync live; ≥ 1 Premium chain running TeachLite inside Google Classroom.

Position JioPC as the household study station for school-age kids. Pre-load the Embibe student app, AI tutor, and homework client. Bundle pricing into the Jio Education school SKUs so parents pay a single monthly amount that includes home learning.
**Segments:** All (highest leverage in Affordable + Mid where households don't have a dedicated PC) · **Pillar:** Learn at Home

- Affordable / Mid households get a study computer without buying hardware — TV + keyboard + ₹599/month.
- Done when: Embibe + AI Tutor live on JioPC; bundled pricing finalised; ≥ 50K JioPC subscribers reachable via Embibe.

**Jio's position:**
- **OCR grading is a category-first feature.** No competitor offers it fully — Extramarks has a partial implementation, every other player has nothing. If TeachLite 2.0 ships OCR + AI grading, it is the only platform in India K-12 to do so.
- **Embibe's content library is the hardest asset to replicate.** Every competitor with a content library is locked to their own ecosystem (LEAD-only, Tata-only). Embibe's 1.8 M Q-bank across 29 boards and 11 languages has no equivalent at this breadth.
- **Teachmint X is the AI benchmark.** They lead on AI lesson generation, equation recognition, and voice assistant — features absent from Superr's current build. TeachLite 2.0 must match Teachmint X on AI delivery features to compete at the same tier. Project D2 (IFPD enhancement) closes this gap.

- **Google Classroom is a complementary LMS, not a board platform.** It has no native whiteboard and no hardware. Schools running Google for Education still need a board platform — TeachLite 2.0 can coexist (A4) or replace depending on the school's dependency on Google Workspace.

## Appendix B — Personas

Four personas. If any of them disengages, the stack fails. The homework loop closes only if all four act on it — teacher assigns, student does the work, parent sees progress, principal reports up. **The teacher app does not exist today. That is the single biggest product gap.**

| Persona | Job 1 — daily | Job 2 — episodic | Where we serve them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student | Prep for tomorrow's class on any device, in the right language | After every test, see what went wrong and how to fix it | Book, Embibe app, student app (SIM), JioPC at home |
| Teacher | Cut prep and grading time — get evenings back | Spot which students are falling behind on a concept, and act | TeachLite 2.0, Board, **teacher app (missing)** |
| Principal / Admin | One dashboard for fees, attendance, outcomes, NEP/PARAKH across all schools | Full picture on a child in under a minute when a parent escalates | DigiCampus, Admin Dashboard |
| Parent | One bill, one app, one view of how my child is doing | Help with homework I don't understand | Parent app (SIM), Jio Pay |
